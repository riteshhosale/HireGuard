## ❓ Why HireGuard

Fake job postings, fake recruiters, registration-fee scams, and phishing "onboarding" portals are a growing problem for job seekers — especially students and first-time applicants. Most people have no easy way to check whether a recruiter or hiring site is legitimate.

HireGuard gives job seekers a single place to:

1. **Scan** a suspicious website or job URL.
2. **See evidence** behind the risk verdict — not just a score.
3. **Ask AI** to interpret suspicious patterns in plain language.
4. **Share** scam experiences with a community so others don't fall for the same trap.

## 📌 Overview

HireGuard AI is a full-stack security platform that helps job seekers investigate suspicious recruitment websites and job offers.

Core flow:

```text
Suspicious Job Offer
        ↓
Suspicious Website / URL
        ↓
HireGuard AI Scan
        ↓
Evidence Collection
        ↓
Risk Analysis
        ↓
AI-Assisted Insights
        ↓
Security Report
        ↓
Optional Community Scam Report
